## What Legacy Wolf is
A monitoring-and-response service for people living solo. A quiet operator running in the background that activates if you don't — executing the instructions you set, so your last impression is the one you wanted to leave.

**Brand promise:** *Sixty more minutes of control — exactly when you can't be there.*
**Signature sentence shape:** *You instruct us to…* — the user is the author; we are the executor.
**Category:** sits alongside wills, advance directives, and life insurance — and acts in the minutes and hours after you can't.

## The four services
| Nav name | Card name | One line |
|---|---|---|
| Friend Alert | Just Call your Friend or Police | We contact your nominated friend (or police) to check on you. |
| Attend Check | Attend and Check on You | A Security-Accredited Agent — a Registered Nurse with animal-care training — attends your address using your encrypted access details. |
| Pet Guardian | Help your Pets | Immediate on-site pet care, then handover to your nominated guardian. |
| Reputation Guard | Pants On Service | We follow your instructions for what to clean, hide, or remove before family arrive. |

"Pants On Service" is a protected equity asset — never sanitize it away.

## Audience
People living solo — mindset over demographics: practical, unflinching, quietly amused by the absurdity, the kind of person who already has a will. Not for novelty-app users, gamification seekers, or wellness-tracker shoppers.

## Personality
**Archetype:** the Outfitter for the Inevitable — Guardian + Quiet Operator.
**Lean into:** composed, direct, prepared, dignified, quietly witty, unshockable.
**Reject:** cute, childlike, cheerful-by-default, salesy, sentimentalized, cluttered.
The wolf is a loyal guardian — never fierce, never wild.

## Voice — two registers, one voice
- **Outside voice** (marketing, landing, social, ads): direct, witty, names the thing. "Don't be found three days late."
- **Inside voice** (every product surface: onboarding, settings, notifications, errors, post-event comms): measured, precise, plainspoken, never alarming. "If you don't check in within this time, we'll begin the response you authorized."

Rules that bind both: name the thing (say "died", never "rainbow bridge"); adult to adult; outcome over fear; equal weight to humans, pets, and dignity; the user is the author; wit only when it earns its keep; never cute, never childlike.

**Words we use:** instruct, control, attend, respond, monitor, prepared, guardian, dignity, nominated, encrypted, accredited, legacy, solo, in-between.
**Words we never use:** fur baby, doggo, paw-some, beloved, sacred, journey (as euphemism), rainbow bridge, passed away (use "died").

## What Legacy Wolf is NOT
Not pet insurance, pet GPS, a pet social network, a vet-finder, a wellness app, a will service, a fear-marketing product, or a cute-pet brand. If an asset drifts toward any of these — stop.

## Trust signals (use specifically, never generically)
Australian National Character Check vetting · Registered Nurses with compassionate animal-care training · Encrypted access details · *You instruct, we execute.*
